# CapCut Assembly Notes

How the twelve scenes were assembled into the finished piece.

## Timeline

1. Import the twelve scene clips in order (`scenes/scene01/` … `scenes/scene12/`).
2. Each clip is ~10 seconds; trim heads/tails where the motion settles.
3. Export target: **1920×1080, 24fps, 16:9**.

## Audio layers

- **Mute each clip's built-in audio track** (or drop it very low). The generator's synthesized sound can carry garbled voice-like noise that fights the real dialogue.
- Layer the per-scene dialogue MP3s (`scenes/sceneNN/*-dialogue.mp3`) on a dedicated voice track. Nudge timing so lines land on the visible speaking beats — true lip sync isn't possible, so aim for plausible alignment, especially in close-ups.
- Scene 6: Zei's whispered aside ("The new room feels safer.") sits lower in the mix.
- Scene 8: hold a beat of room tone after "Exactly."
- Scene 9: let the silence after "You've…" breathe — the students' shared smiles finish the beat.
- Scene 12: short silence after "Dzego will rise again," then the restrained low musical note.

## Graphics and text (added in CapCut, never generated)

- Poster motto and any on-screen wording.
- Watch graphics (the watch faces were kept abstract in the clips — simple yellow verification symbol, no readable text).
- Subtitles if desired.
- The gas-jar diagram labels for scenes 7–8 (the projection was generated word/number-free deliberately).

## Music and ambience

- No music under dialogue as a rule; ambience per the audio notes in each scene prompt.
- The final low musical note in scene 12 is added here, not generated.

## Color

All clips share the controlled palette (leaf green, oxidized teal, warm amber, concrete gray) and subtle film grain from the common visual prompt, so grading should be light — match blacks and skin tones across the street (daylight) and classroom (practical green-blue) scenes.
