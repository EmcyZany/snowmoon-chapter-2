# Scene Prompts (1–12)

The base prompts below are the shot plan. During production, each was sent to the video generator **verbatim**, with the common visual prompt appended, plus a short context block that:

1. identified each attached reference image (environment vs. each character),
2. directed **subtle, restrained speaking motion** for dialogue scenes (small natural lip movement, never exaggerated),
3. for multi-character scenes, explicitly required the characters to remain **clearly different people** (no blended/morphed features),
4. applied per-scene framing fixes (e.g. scene 7: instructor's face visible asking; scene 8: Zei's face visible answering; scene 11: no students touching watches).

Dialogue was recorded separately (see `dialogue/full-script.md` and `docs/voice-guide.md`) — the dialogue lines in the prompts describe the intended performance for the generator's own synthesized sound, not the final audio.

## 1. Hun Min Street — establish the world

Attach: Hun Min Street environment reference; Fin and Zei references if the characters appear.

```
Wide establishing shot of Hun Min Street in Pafogai Du, Dzego, in daylight. Camera slowly glides forward at pedestrian height beneath leafy street trees. Small electronics shops and miniature workshops glow with colored lights; workers assemble tiny devices behind open windows; stairways descend underground. Fin and Zei step down from a modest public autobus in the middle distance and begin walking along the sidewalk. Lived-in, inventive, quietly watchful city; avoid futuristic spectacle. No readable shop signs.

Audio: layered street ambience—soft autobus brake hiss, footsteps, distant workshop tools, gentle electronic shop chimes, leaves moving in a light breeze. No music, no intelligible background voices, no dialogue.
```

## 2. Fin and Zei talk as they walk

Attach: Fin + Zei + Hun Min Street references.

```
Medium two-shot tracking backward in front of Fin and Zei as they walk briskly beneath the trees along Hun Min Street. Fin glances toward Zei with friendly curiosity; Zei answers with a small proud smile, using one hand to describe a strategy from their Minpentai game. Natural conversational gestures, restrained expressions, keep their faces and clothes identical to the references. Shop lights and workshop windows drift softly behind them.

Dialogue, Fin, warm and curious: “How did your game go?”
Dialogue, Zei, pleased but understated: “Third place. I found a way to turn their gliders back on them.”
Dialogue, Fin, lightly teasing: “And I spent the evening studying geography.”

Audio: quiet footsteps, distant electronics shops, soft tree leaves. Voices close and clear, natural conversational pace. No music.
```

## 3. The security drone passes

Attach: Fin + Zei + Hun Min Street references.

```
Low-angle shot from behind Fin and Zei as a small ordinary-looking security drone crosses above the tree-lined street. Tilt up to follow it briefly, then return to the two students. Their wristwatches give one subtle synchronized pulse; both barely notice and continue walking. The drone should feel routine, not threatening. Keep the same faces, clothes and watches as the references.

Audio: brief soft rotor buzz passing overhead, a tiny paired watch chime, then normal street ambience resumes. No alarm, no ominous music, no dialogue.
```

## 4. The changed lesson location

Attach: Fin + Zei + Hun Min Street references.

```
Close-up of Zei checking the watch on his left wrist while walking; show a simple abstract yellow verification symbol, not readable text. Rack focus to Fin looking at the watch, then to Zei thinking through the route. Zei points down a side street; the two turn and walk faster. Their behavior is alert but ordinary, not panicked. Keep the same character designs.

Dialogue, Zei, low and practical: “The lesson location changed. We need to sync the new proof.”
Dialogue, Fin: “Is it far?”
Dialogue, Zei: “Close enough. Let’s move.”

Audio: quiet watch buzz, two quick footsteps accelerating, distant street hum. Dialogue clean and understated; no other audible voices.
```

Production note: regenerated once with subtler speaking-motion direction after the first version's lip movement looked off.

## 5. The last-minute room change

Attach: Fin + Zei + classroom or stairwell reference.

```
Handheld but steady follow shot as Fin and Zei descend two dusty flights of stairs toward a hidden classroom. A young woman waiting on the landing recognizes Fin and gestures them onward. At the bottom, a male organizer calmly stops the group and redirects them to a different room one level up. Fin and Zei exchange a brief uncertain glance, then follow without arguing. Modest concrete stairwell, practical lighting, no dramatic spy gadgets.

Dialogue, organizer, calm and brisk: “Change of room. Upstairs, opposite side.”
Dialogue, Zei, quiet sigh: “Again?”
Dialogue, organizer: “This way.”

Audio: stairwell footsteps with a soft echo, distant muffled classroom murmur, one door opening and closing. Voices natural and low; no music.
```

## 6. Inside the improvised secure classroom

Attach: Fin + Zei + classroom references; add the instructor reference only if he appears.

```
Slow wide pan across the modest Dzego physics classroom. Reflective anti-transmission foil covers the walls beneath cheerful drawings of cartoon animals, gadgets and chemistry equipment. At the front, a cartoon pig holds a calculator and a hamster holds a vial; leave the poster lettering blank and indistinct. Fin and Zei sit among students. One student casually checks beneath the table edge; another sweeps a hand device around the room. Their checks are habitual and discreet, not melodramatic. The instructor enters and closes the door.

Dialogue, instructor, patient and welcoming: “Welcome. Today we’re looking at thermodynamics.”
Dialogue, Zei, very softly to Fin: “The new room feels safer.”
Fin gives a small nod; no spoken reply.

Audio: low classroom room tone, chair legs shifting, foil lightly crinkling as the door closes, soft watch tap. Keep dialogue crisp; no music.
```

## 7. The two jars: entropy made visual

Attach: instructor + classroom references; use Zei reference if he is visible.

```
Over-the-shoulder view from behind the instructor toward the class. A simple classroom projection shows two transparent gas jars: one with fast-moving glowing particles, one with slower particles. The particles should be beautiful but scientifically legible, not magical. Cut briefly to Zei listening closely. The instructor points from the faster jar to the slower one. Do not render any words or numbers on the screen; add graphics later in CapCut.

Dialogue, instructor, measured and clear: “One jar is hot, one is cool. If we combine them, what can we still know?”
Dialogue, Zei, attentive: “The particles mix. Their motion settles somewhere between the two.”

Audio: quiet classroom ambience, faint projector hum, very subtle soft particle-like ticks that rise and fall with the animation. No music under speech.
```

Production note: regenerated once — reframed so the instructor's **face is visible** as he asks the question (three-quarter front angle instead of from-behind).

## 8. The lesson's idea lands

Attach: instructor + Zei + classroom references.

```
Begin on the two projected jars as their particles mix into one shared space. The separate patterns become a more uniform, busy field. Cut to the instructor, then a close-up of Zei as he understands the connection between heat, uncertainty and information. Students lean forward. Use a restrained visual metaphor: the image becomes harder to describe as the particles mix, without turning into a digital interface or fantasy effect.

Dialogue, instructor: “After they mix, we know less about the exact motion of each particle.”
Dialogue, Zei, thinking aloud: “So the missing information has increased.”
Dialogue, instructor, approving: “Exactly.”

Audio: soft projector hum, subtle particle movement, room tone. Let the word “Exactly” land with a short pause afterward. No music.
```

Production note: regenerated once — the first version muddled the two characters' features, so the redo carried an explicit "two clearly different people, do not blend features" direction, with Zei's face shown answering.

## 9. A shared moment before the blast

Attach: Zei + instructor + classroom references.

```
Intimate classroom two-shot: Zei has just offered a clever answer. The instructor starts to praise him, while several students share a small knowing smile at the lesson’s quiet political implication. Zei chooses not to explain the joke out loud. Hold for a beat on the students’ brief, restrained moment of shared understanding. Keep the humor subtle and humane.

Dialogue, instructor, beginning warmly: “That’s a strong explanation, Zei. You’ve—”
No one interrupts. Let the shared smiles and silence finish the beat.

Audio: faint room tone, a tiny chair creak, subdued breath and a soft almost-laugh from one student. No music. End with a short pause.
```

## 10. The nearby blast and classroom damage

Attach: Fin + Zei + classroom references.

```
Continue in the same classroom. A sudden heavy impact erupts from a nearby room, not inside this classroom. The camera jolts once; dust drops from the ceiling; part of the wall and floor crack and collapse inward with chunks of plaster and stone. Chairs topple. Zei is knocked down and a small piece of stone strikes his left arm; show no blood or graphic injury. Fin is thrown off balance but remains visibly unhurt. Do not show an attacker, weapon, bomb, or exterior explosion. Keep the event sudden and plausible.

Audio: abrupt low-frequency boom from nearby, sharp structural crack, falling rubble, brief muffled ringing, dust and debris settling. No screams, no music, no extra dialogue during the impact.
```

Production note: regenerated once to include the full classroom of classmates at their desks when the impact hits.

## 11. Fin helps Zei; the danger becomes real

Attach: Fin + Zei + classroom references.

```
Dusty, close handheld shot at floor level. Zei sits dazed beside his fallen chair and tries to lift his left arm, but it does not respond. Fin steps into frame, checks Zei’s face, and carefully supports him upright into a chair. The instructor moves through the smoky room checking students. Watches begin to beep, then students tap them to silence the alarms. Show concern and teamwork, not panic or graphic injury.

Dialogue, instructor, calling to the room: “Is anyone hurt? Tell me now.”
Dialogue, Fin, steady and reassuring to Zei: “I’ve got you. Stay still.”
Dialogue, Zei, quiet and shaken: “The room change saved us.”

Audio: dust settling, several air-quality watch beeps that stop one by one, quiet concerned movement, filtered breathing. Keep speech close and intelligible; no music.
```

Production note: regenerated once — no students touching their watches, each voice recorded separately, stronger distinct faces/expressions.

## 12. The final line

Attach: Fin + Zei + instructor references; use classroom reference showing light damage.

```
Quiet final medium shot in the damaged classroom. The instructor checks Zei’s left arm with a hand device while Fin remains beside him. Students help one another up in the soft haze. Zei and Fin look at each other, scared but composed, then speak together with calm resolve. End on the foil-covered wall and the cheerful animal poster, now dusted with rubble; slowly fade out. Do not show a villain or add an action-movie victory pose.

Dialogue, instructor, concerned but composed: “That arm needs a hospital today.”
Dialogue, Fin: “I’ll take him.”
Dialogue, Fin and Zei together, quiet and defiant: “Dzego will rise again.”

Audio: the last few watch beeps fade, dust drifting, subdued room tone. Leave a short silence after the final line, then a restrained low musical note that can be added in CapCut.
```

## Voice consistency notes (from the original plan)

If you generate the voices separately, keep these descriptions in your production notes and reuse the same voice settings on every line:

```
FIN: Young male voice, warm mid-range, open and friendly, quick but not rushed, clear English, natural conversational delivery, no exaggerated accent, no theatrical acting.

ZEI: Young male voice, slightly lower and more measured than Fin, thoughtful, dry humor, calm under pressure, clear English, restrained emotion rather than a heroic tone.

INSTRUCTOR: Adult male voice, calm mid-low register, patient teacherly rhythm, clear enunciation, reassuring during the emergency without shouting excessively.

ORGANIZER: Adult voice, practical and composed, concise delivery, gives the room change as routine information rather than a dramatic warning.
```

The concrete voice IDs used in this production are mapped in `docs/voice-guide.md`.

## General generation advice (from the original plan)

- Make reference images first, then generate one clip at a time with the relevant references.
- If a character drifts, regenerate that clip with the clearest character image attached and a simpler camera move.
- In CapCut, add the final poster motto, watch graphics, subtitles if desired, music, and any voice lines that weren't generated cleanly.
- Never ask the generator to render readable signs, watch interfaces, or posters — add important wording later in CapCut; generated text is unreliable.
- Plan for one ~8-second clip per scene; available lengths vary by model, so check the active model's controls before committing to the full set.
