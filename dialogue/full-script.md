# Full Dialogue Script — Snowmoon Chapter 2 Adaptation

All dialogue was newly written for this adaptation. Each character keeps one fixed voice across all scenes (see `docs/voice-guide.md`). Lines were synthesized individually and joined with short pauses; the final unison line was recorded per-voice and mixed.

## Scene 1 — Hun Min Street (establishing)
*No dialogue.*

## Scene 2 — Fin and Zei talk as they walk
- **Fin:** "How did your game go?"
- **Zei:** "Third place. I found a way to turn their gliders back on them."
- **Fin:** "And I spent the evening studying geography."

## Scene 3 — The security drone passes
*No dialogue.*

## Scene 4 — The changed lesson location
- **Zei** *(low and practical):* "The lesson location changed. We need to sync the new proof."
- **Fin:** "Is it far?"
- **Zei:** "Close enough. Let's move."

## Scene 5 — The last-minute room change
- **Organizer** *(calm and brisk):* "Change of room. Upstairs, opposite side."
- **Zei** *(quiet sigh):* "Again?"
- **Organizer:** "This way."

## Scene 6 — Inside the improvised secure classroom
- **Instructor** *(patient and welcoming):* "Welcome. Today we're looking at thermodynamics."
- **Zei** *(very softly, to Fin):* "The new room feels safer."
- **Fin:** *(small nod; no spoken reply)*

## Scene 7 — The two jars: entropy made visual
- **Instructor** *(measured and clear):* "One jar is hot, one is cool. If we combine them, what can we still know?"
- **Zei** *(attentive):* "The particles mix. Their motion settles somewhere between the two."

## Scene 8 — The lesson's idea lands
- **Instructor:** "After they mix, we know less about the exact motion of each particle."
- **Zei** *(thinking aloud):* "So the missing information has increased."
- **Instructor** *(approving):* "Exactly."

## Scene 9 — A shared moment before the blast
- **Instructor** *(beginning warmly, trailing off):* "That's a strong explanation, Zei. You've…"
- *(No one interrupts. Shared smiles and silence finish the beat.)*

## Scene 10 — The nearby blast and classroom damage
*No dialogue. No screams.*

## Scene 11 — Fin helps Zei; the danger becomes real
- **Instructor** *(calling to the room):* "Is anyone hurt? Tell me now."
- **Fin** *(steady and reassuring, to Zei):* "I've got you. Stay still."
- **Zei** *(quiet and shaken):* "The room change saved us."

## Scene 12 — The final line
- **Instructor** *(concerned but composed):* "That arm needs a hospital today."
- **Fin:** "I'll take him."
- **Fin and Zei together** *(quiet and defiant):* "Dzego will rise again."
